**Core thesis:** minimal ≠ good. The bare/unstyled markdown sites we looked at (bshlgrs, colah, neelnanda, karpathy, rohinshah) all rated ugly precisely because they lacked typographic craft, not because they were too minimal. Restraint has to be paired with actual design decisions (type, color, spacing), or it just reads as neglect.

**Structure:** Home, Research, Creative. Research and Creative share one reusable entry format: title, one-line description, date/tag, link, plus a compact thumbnail. Revised from the original "no cards, no thumbnails" call — thumbnails read better in practice, just kept small rather than full-width/image-heavy.

**Voice:** methods-first, no overselling, matches your existing stated preference. Voice should carry a slightly poetic feel for language, used deliberately to show personality."

**Base reference:** kaustubh.ml. Casual first-person copy, minimal info density, paper-esque restraint. Explicitly not the webring part of that site.

**Color:** one accent on white/black.

**Type:** serif headers (confirmed good after you reverted the full cursive experiment), plain body type. Cormorant Garamond family came up as a candidate via the title-page reference, not locked in as final.

**Motion:** Josh Comeau level of polish and intentionality, but scoped to a few placements, not everywhere. Rauno Freiberg's technique was too much applied everywhere, that's the specific failure mode to avoid.

**Handwritten layer:** sketchy annotations (Rough Notation style, underlines/circles/highlights on specific words, likely hover-triggered), not full doodle illustration or a cursive font swap. Maggie Appleton's hand-drawn diagrams are the model, but only where you're actually explaining something, not as page decoration. Full-doodle (ncase.me) and full-script-font headers were both rejected as too much.

**Signature piece:** one contained interactive element, scoped to a section of the Creative page, not a full-page takeover (bruno-simon.com confirmed too much). Currently prototyping toward an ASCII-rendered 3D object as the concept.

**Icons:** outline/negative style, sketchy treatment via RoughJS over clean base SVGs (Simple Icons), for consistency with the Rough Notation mechanism rather than a separate hand-drawn style.

**Tags/filtering:** cut from ~23 tags down to a handful of broad ones (currently Research/Philosophy/AI/Music). Plain text, no pill/button chrome on individual entries. Filter control is a persistent row of text tabs (underline on the active one), not a type-to-filter input — simpler and more discoverable for a small tag set.

**Explicitly rejected:** full unstyled markdown (too bare), Rauno-level omnipresent interaction (too much), Bruno Simon-scale takeover (too much), brittanychiang.com's dark-portfolio format (competent but no personality), full doodle-character illustration (ncase.me, too doodley), all-cursive type system (too much of the handwritten thing in one move).
